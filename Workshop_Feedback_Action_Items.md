# Workshop Feedback & Action Items

## Lost and Found for Campus --- Team Falcons

## Authentication

-   Require authenticated user accounts.
-   Use email/password for the initial implementation.
-   Evaluate UNT SSO as a future integration.

## Listing Lifecycle

-   Define Active, Potential Match, Claim Pending, Resolved, and
    Archived states.
-   Add a reopening process for incorrectly resolved listings.
-   Consider reminders/archival rules for inactive listings.

## Claim Verification & Privacy

-   Keep claim verification separate from messaging.
-   Define public versus hidden identifying information.
-   Open messaging only after claim approval.

## Messaging

-   Define report and block options.
-   Allow admins to review reported conversations.

## Location

-   Use building + zone.
-   For large buildings, define recognizable zones such as entrance,
    floor/wing, common area, and parking area.

## Photos

-   Support photo upload as a core requirement.
-   Consider mobile camera capture as an enhancement.

## Matching Engine

Potential matches consider category, location, date proximity, and
description keywords. Final scoring and thresholds will be defined
during system design.

## Handoff

Evaluate direct handoff through approved messaging and an optional
designated campus Lost and Found center.

## Third-Party Services

Test integrations early, monitor free-tier usage, and keep fallback
options.

## Admin Usability

Design routine management for nontechnical staff and separate
operational work from technical maintenance.

## Scope

Protect core listings, authentication, search/filter, and resolution
workflow first. Simplify stretch features if the team falls behind.
